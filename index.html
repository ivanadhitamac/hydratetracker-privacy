Privacy Policy — HydrateTracker

Effective date: March 31, 2026

HydrateTracker is designed to respect your privacy.

Data we collect

HydrateTracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data from you.

Data stored on your device

Any information you enter in the app (such as your hydration logs and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No third-party SDKs used to collect data

Internet access

HydrateTracker does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

Sharing your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties because we do not collect it.

Children’s privacy

HydrateTracker does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not collect data, we do not process children’s personal data.

Changes to this policy

If we change our privacy practices, we will update this privacy policy and revise the effective date above.
